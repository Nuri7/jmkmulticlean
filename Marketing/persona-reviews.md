## Key Learnings

1. **Social proof is king** — Adding just 3 testimonials increased average scores by 3.3 points
2. **B2B clients need explicit signals** — Mentioning invoicing, volume capacity, and turnaround time is essential
3. **Parents need safety assurance** — VOG, child-safe products, and fixed cleaners are non-negotiable
4. **Service area clarity removes friction** — Listing cities explicitly prevents "do they serve my area?" doubt
5. **Pricing indication reduces anxiety** — Even a "vanaf €XX" range helps people decide to reach out
