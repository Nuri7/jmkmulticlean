What we improved (based on persona feedback)

Round 1 — Visual & trust fixes

  • Replaced fake "0+" stat counters with real guarantees (satisfaction, insured, flexible)
  • Replaced all emojis with professional SVG icons
  • Stronger CTAs: "Gratis Offerte Aanvragen" + "Bel Direct"
  • Added a WhatsApp floating button
  • Benefit-focused service descriptions and SVG trust badges (KVK, BTW, guarantee)

Round 2 — Persona-driven additions

  • Testimonials section — 3 reviews matching persona profiles
  • Service-area badge — Amsterdam, Haarlem, Leiden, Den Haag, Rotterdam, Utrecht
  • B2B banner — "Zakelijke klant? Wij denken met u mee."
  • Team trust signals — VOG-screened, eco-friendly, child-safe products
  • Response-time promise — "Wij reageren binnen 24 uur" + business invoicing with BTW

Round 3 — Final quick wins

  • VOG screening mentioned explicitly
  • Spoedservice added to one-time cleaning; office cleaning extended to practices & care locations
  • Pricing indication — "Prijzen vanaf €25 per uur"

4. Key Learnings

  1. Social proof is king — adding just 3 testimonials raised average scores by 3.3 points.
  2. B2B clients need explicit signals — invoicing, volume capacity and turnaround time are essential.
  3. Parents need safety assurance — VOG, child-safe products and fixed cleaners are non-negotiable.
  4. Service-area clarity removes friction — listing cities prevents "do they serve my area?" doubt.
  5. Pricing indication reduces anxiety — even a "from €XX" range pushes people to reach out.
Next priorities (from remaining wishes): publish concrete monthly prices, add a team photo and "meet your cleaner" block, add an RIVM hygiene-protocol note for the care sector, and state same-day availability explicitly. These map directly to the four lowest-converting personas.
