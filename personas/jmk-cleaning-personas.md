# JMK Cleaning Services - Target Personas

## Persona 1: Lisa – The Busy Professional Homeowner

**Name:** Lisa de Vries  
**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Marketing Manager at a tech company  
**Location:** Amsterdam area  
**Family:** Lives with her partner in a recently renovated apartment

### Profile
Lisa works long hours and travels frequently for work. She and her partner recently bought and renovated an apartment. She values her free time and wants a clean, fresh home without spending her weekends cleaning.

### Goals
- Find a reliable cleaning service for regular or one-time deep cleaning
- Get high-quality results without having to manage everything herself
- Feel confident that the cleaners are professional and trustworthy

### Pain Points
- No time to clean properly herself
- Had bad experiences with unreliable cleaners in the past
- Wants someone who communicates clearly and respects her home

### Why She Visits the Website
- Looking for professional cleaning after renovation
- Wants to see services, prices (or clear process), and reviews
- Needs to feel that the company is legitimate and insured

### What Matters Most to Her
- Reliability and communication
- Quality of work
- Professionalism and insurance
- Easy booking process

## Persona 2: Mark – The Property Manager / Landlord

**Name:** Mark Janssen  
**Age:** 48  
**Occupation:** Property manager / Landlord (owns 12 rental properties)
**Location:** Utrecht region

### Profile
Mark manages multiple rental apartments. He regularly needs cleaning between tenants, especially after people move out. He works with several cleaning companies but is always looking for more reliable and consistent partners.

### Goals
- Find a cleaning company that can handle turnovers quickly and thoroughly
- Get consistent quality across different properties
- Build a long-term relationship with one reliable provider

### Pain Points
- Many cleaners are unreliable or deliver inconsistent quality
- Needs fast turnaround between tenants
- Wants clear communication and proper invoicing

### Why He Visits the Website
- Looking for a professional partner for regular turnover cleaning
- Wants to see if the company can handle multiple properties
- Checks for KVK registration, insurance, and professionalism

### What Matters Most to Him
- Reliability and speed
- Consistent quality
- Professional communication and invoicing
- Scalability (can they handle multiple properties?)

## Persona 3: Sarah – The Small Business Owner

**Name:** Sarah Bakker  
**Age:** 41  
**Occupation:** Owner of a physiotherapy practice (3 treatment rooms)
**Location:** Rotterdam

### Profile
Sarah runs a small physiotherapy clinic. She wants her practice to always look clean and professional because it directly affects how patients perceive her business. She currently cleans herself or uses a cheap cleaner, but the quality is inconsistent.

### Goals
- Keep her practice clean and hygienic at all times
- Find a reliable cleaner who understands medical/professional environments
- Reduce the time she spends on cleaning herself

### Pain Points
- Current cleaner is not consistent
- She spends too much of her own time cleaning
- Wants someone who pays attention to hygiene and detail

### Why She Visits the Website
- Looking for regular cleaning for her practice
- Wants to know if the company has experience with small businesses/offices
- Checks reviews and professionalism

### What Matters Most to Her
- Cleanliness and hygiene standards
- Reliability
- Professional appearance of the cleaner(s)
- Good communication

## Persona 4: Thomas – The Real Estate Agent

**Name:** Thomas van der Meer  
**Age:** 36  
**Occupation:** Real Estate Agent (Makelaar)
**Location:** The Hague / Delft area

### Profile
Thomas sells and rents out homes. He frequently needs cleaning services before viewings, after renovations, or when preparing a house for sale. A clean house makes a much better impression on potential buyers.

### Goals
- Quick and high-quality cleaning before important viewings
- Make properties look their best to increase chances of selling/renting
- Have a reliable cleaner he can call on short notice

### Pain Points
- Needs fast service, sometimes on short notice
- Previous cleaners were not detail-oriented enough
- Wants the property to look fresh and well-maintained

### Why He Visits the Website
- Looking for a cleaning company for pre-viewing or post-renovation cleaning
- Wants to see if they can deliver fast and high-quality results
- Checks professionalism and reliability

### What Matters Most to Him
- Speed and flexibility
- Quality of the end result (especially visual appeal)
- Ability to work on short notice
- Professionalism

## Persona 5: Anita – The Working Mother

**Name:** Anita Patel  
**Age:** 39  
**Occupation:** HR Advisor (part-time)
**Location:** Eindhoven area  
**Family:** Married with two young children (ages 4 and 7)

### Profile
Anita works part-time and has two young children. Between work, school runs, and family life, she has very little time left for thorough cleaning. She wants help with regular cleaning so she can spend more quality time with her family instead of cleaning on weekends.

### Goals
- Get help with weekly or bi-weekly house cleaning
- Reduce stress and create more family time
- Find someone she can trust in her home with her children around

### Pain Points
- Feels overwhelmed by household tasks
- Wants a cleaner who is trustworthy around her kids
- Previous experiences with unreliable or low-quality cleaners

### Why She Visits the Website
- Looking for regular cleaning help
- Wants to feel confident about safety and trustworthiness
- Checks for reviews, guarantees, and professionalism

### What Matters Most to Her
- Trust and safety (especially with children at home)
- Reliability and consistency
- Friendly and respectful cleaners
- Good communication
